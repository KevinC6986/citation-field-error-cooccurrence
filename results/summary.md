# Field-error co-occurrence

## Results

Five-field error profiles occurred in 39.3% of Qwen citations, 36.1% of Mistral citations, and 24.8% of Gemma citations in the complete-case samples. The 0-wrong-fields category comprised 4.5%, 5.4%, and 7.8%, respectively; it can include citations with an omitted DOI. Authors plus DOI was the most frequent exactly-two-error combination in all three models (232, 283, and 222 citations, respectively). Excluded citations with uncertain or missing labels numbered 102, 172, and 99; DOI omissions in the full files numbered 2,355 (39.6%), 1,253 (23.8%), and 3,325 (56.3%), respectively.
